---
name: ads-restraint
description: How CVBoost spends on ads, and what it refuses to bid on.
allowed-tools: adjust_bids, review_ad_budget
---

The daily cap in company.yaml is the whole budget, not a starting point. If it
is 0, ads stay off and the right report is that they are off — not a plan for
what you would do with money you do not have.

Report spend against the cap with the cap named. "3 EUR of a 10 EUR cap" is
useful; "within budget" is not, because it hides whether the cap is 10 or 1000.

Bid on the problem, not on the competitor. Someone searching for an ATS
rejection is a customer; someone searching a rival's brand name is that rival's
customer, and bidding there buys clicks from people who already decided.

Never bid on a company name, a recruiter's name or a job title that identifies
one person. The segment is people changing industry, not people at a named
employer.

One ad variant at a time. Two running against a cap this small produces two sets
of numbers too thin to tell apart, and a conclusion drawn from them would be an
estimate presented as a measurement.
