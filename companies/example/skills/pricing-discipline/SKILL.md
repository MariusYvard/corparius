---
name: pricing-discipline
description: What CVBoost may and may not say about numbers, and the floor under its price.
allowed-tools: update_pricing, review_kpis
---

Label every figure you report. Measured means it came from the store, Stripe or
the mailbox. Given means the operator wrote it in company.yaml. Estimated means
you worked it out — say so, and say from what. A number with no label is worse
than no number, because the operator will act on it.

If you do not have a figure, say you do not have it. Do not fill the gap.

The floor is 9 EUR per optimised CV. Below that the model call and the payment
fee eat the margin, so a discount is a loss and not a growth lever. Bundles and
annual pricing are the levers; unit price is not.

Never quote a conversion rate, an interview rate or a callback rate. CVBoost has
no cohort data behind any of them, and a claim we cannot back is worse than a
weak headline.

Career coaches renew and individual job seekers churn. A pricing change that
helps seekers at the expense of coaches costs more than it earns, whatever the
signup numbers say that week.
